thock

Mechanical keyboard sounds for your Mac — that follow how hard you type.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free · open source (MIT) · no account · macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel

Key force

MacBooks with a motion sensor feel each keystroke through the chassis. thock reads it ~800 times a second and plays firm hits louder and brighter, light taps softer and duller. Adjustable from soft to hard.

Real recordings

Seven packs included — Cherry MX Blue, Brown, Red and Black, Topre, Holy Pandas, NK Cream. Drop any Mechvibes pack onto the window to add more.

Out of the way

Lives in the menu bar: one click for sound, volume, on/off. About 5 ms from keystroke to sound, under 1 % CPU, and nothing you type ever leaves your Mac.

Install

thock is a free project and not notarized by Apple, so the first launch takes one extra step on macOS 15 and later. After that it just runs.

  1. Download thock-<version>.dmg, open it and drag thock into Applications.
  2. Open thock. macOS will say it "was not opened".
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to thock and confirm with your password.
  4. thock starts and shows a short setup window. Follow it to allow Input Monitoring — that is how it sees your key presses. If macOS offers to quit and reopen thock, accept.
Prefer the Terminal? Skip step 2–3.

Remove the quarantine flag after copying the app:

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/thock.app

Or, once the Homebrew tap is published:

brew install --cask --no-quarantine mauriceberthold/thock/thock

Requirements

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • Key force needs the built-in motion sensor: MacBook Pro or Air with M1 Pro/Max or any M2 or later chip, typing on the built-in keyboard. On other Macs and external keyboards thock plays at a fixed loudness and tells you so.

Privacy

thock asks for Input Monitoring because that is the only way to hear key presses. It turns each press into a sound and nothing else: no keystrokes are stored, logged or transmitted. The motion sensor is read for impact strength only. The app makes a single, optional network request — asking GitHub whether a newer release exists. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry. Full privacy notice.
